Part 1

Attendance

One entry per person per working day. It is the record payroll reads, the record your lead reads, and the only one that exists.

1.1

What counts as a record

The rule

Attendance is recorded only through Staff Hub → Attendance in GoodDay. A message in chat, a note in a call, or a word to your lead is not a record and will not be counted.

Two forms, one of them each day. Working, you file a Daily Standup. Not working, a Time Off Request. Never both. Sundays are the exception: they are off by default, so you file nothing.

Your dayWhat you fileWho answers
WorkingDaily Standup, before you startNobody. Filing it is the mark
SundayNothing. It is a fixed day offNobody
SickTime Off Request, same morningJulius
Taking a day offTime Off Request, 2 working days aheadJulius
1.2

Every working day

The rule

One Daily Standup for every working day, Monday to Saturday, before you start. No standup on a Sunday. The data does three jobs: the presence record, workload visibility for your lead, and capacity planning across the team.

In GoodDay
Pick yourself, not the person next to you

If somebody else's screen is open, the form will happily file your day against them. Check the name before you submit.

Nobody asks how heavy your day feels

The old Heavy / Medium / Light / Standby / OFF rating is gone. How loaded you are is read from the jobs on the board and from what you write here, not from a number you give yourself. If you are under water, put it in Blocked by anything? or tell your lead directly.

1.3
If you see Form Designer, you are in the wrong place

Attendance also has tabs called Daily Standup and Time Off Request. For most people those open the same form. For anyone with full access, which means Rashy and Julius, they open the form designer instead: the same fields, but you are editing the form rather than filling one in, and nothing you type there is recorded.

The giveaway is a grey strip above the form reading Form Designer, Settings, Submissions. If you see it, close it and come back through Quick add. Do not edit the fields, because that changes the form for everyone.

One record, one day

The rule

One standup covers one day. Two standups on the same date read as two days worked and will be corrected.

Worked on two jobs today? The main one goes in Which job? and the rest in Other work today. Missed a day? You can still file it against an earlier date, as long as you are inside the current period. After the 25th you cannot. See 1.5.

1.4

Asking for a day you are not working

The rule

Time off is requested through the Time Off Request form, reached the same way as the standup: Quick add, then Request. A day off needs two working days' notice. Sick can be filed the same morning, with documentation where it is required. One request covers a run of days, so you do not file one per day.

Only Julius can mark a day as paid leave, and payroll reads that field and nothing else. If you believe a day should be paid leave, write it in the request. Do not assume.

1.5

The monthly cycle and the cut-off

The rule

Each attendance period runs from the 26th of the previous month through the 25th of the current month. All entries and corrections must be finalised by the 25th at 23:59 WIB. Records left incomplete after cut-off count as unrecorded, and there is no retroactive correction.

26thperiod opens 25th23:59 locked

Everything inside the bar is still editable. The red line is the lock. Payroll is calculated from what sits behind it.

Check your own month before the deadline. Open All records, find your name, and look for missing dates, wrong dates, and notes too thin to mean anything in three months' time.

There is no catching up afterwards

Anything not submitted by the 25th is not processed. This is the one deadline in the policy with no discretion attached to it.

Part 2

Hours and standby

Flexible by default. Output and deadlines come before fixed schedules, with two windows where the studio needs you reachable.

2.1

Standard working hours

The rule

The expected arrangement averages 8 effective hours per day, or 40 per week, excluding breaks, consistent with the Manpower Law (UU No. 13/2003) as amended. It is a reference point, not a rigid daily target.

We are a remote-first team. Where you work from does not matter. Being reachable when the work needs you does.

2.2

Standby windows

The rule

Because most clients are overseas, team members remain available and responsive during the core collaboration windows below. Reachable means alert and able to answer feedback or turn a quick revision, not merely online.

WindowWhy it exists
09:00 – 15:00 WIBThe studio day. Briefs, internal review, anything that needs two people
18:00 – 22:00 WIBThe overseas overlap. Client feedback lands in this window

Some roles run different standby hours, set in the individual contract. If yours differs, your contract is the one that counts.

2.3

Overtime

The rule

Overtime is by mutual agreement and subject to the legal limits under UU No. 13/2003 Article 78. Statutory overtime entitlements on permanent and fixed-term contracts are governed by Indonesian law and your individual contract, and are honoured in full. The studio's obligations here are not negotiable.

If your workload is consistently past what is reasonable, you are entitled to raise it formally. Submit the evidence, meaning task logs, project timelines or communication records, to your lead and to management.

Raising it triggers a review, not a telling-off

Overtime reporting is operational data. It exists so work can be rebalanced, deadlines moved, or more people put on a job. Nobody is penalised for filing it.

Part 3

Time off and leave

Two separate balances that behave differently. Monthly days off convert to money in the end. Annual leave does not.

3.1

Monthly days off

The rule

Sunday is a fixed day off for everyone. You do not request it and you do not file a standup for it.

Every employee gets 8 days off a month, and Sundays come out of that 8. The rest are yours to request. Nobody takes more than 9 days off in any one month.

So the arithmetic depends on how many Sundays fall in the month:

Month withSundays, automaticLeft to requestHard ceiling
4 Sundays449
5 Sundays539

The ninth day is the one above your normal 8. It exists so a rolled-over day from 3.2 can still be used. It is a ceiling, not an entitlement.

Sunday is off, not on call

The studio runs seven days a week, so jobs still move on a Sunday. That is covered by whoever is working, not by you. You are not expected to answer on your Sunday.

Called in on a day off? If the work runs past 2 effective hours, you get either a replacement day at a time you agree, or money, as set out in your contract. This applies to Sundays too.

3.2

Rollover: the N+2 window

The rule

Unused days off do not expire at the end of the month. They carry for two more, and whatever is left after that is converted to money and paid through payroll.

PeriodStatus
Month NDays off earned but not taken
Month N+1Still available as regular days off
Month N+2Final window to take the remaining days
After N+2Converted to money and included in payroll

The conversion rate is in your contract. In practice Sundays absorb most of the 8, so there is less to roll over than there used to be, and the 9-day ceiling caps how much of it you can spend in one month.

3.3

Annual leave

The rule

Government-mandated annual leave under UU No. 13/2003 Article 79, as amended by UU No. 6/2023. 12 working days per year once you have completed 12 months of continuous service, accruing at 1 day per month of completed service. Below 12 months of service you have not yet reached the full allocation, but accrual begins from your first month.

Up to 4 consecutive days at a time. After that you work at least 5 full working days before the next leave request. Requests go in at least 2 working days ahead, the same as a day off.

Annual leave cannot be cashed out

Unlike monthly days off, unused annual leave expires without monetary conversion, under any circumstances. This follows current Indonesian labour regulations. It is the one balance worth planning ahead for.

3.4

Sick leave

The rule

Illness does not affect your pay and does not reduce your annual leave or day-off balance, provided you notify your direct lead in a timely manner and provide supporting documentation where it is required. This is your right under Indonesian labour law.

File it the same morning on the Time Off Request tab, choose Sick, and attach the doctor's note to the request if you have one.

3.5

Unplanned absence

The rule

In all cases of unplanned absence, notify your direct lead or management at the earliest opportunity, including at the last minute. For unplanned personal absences outside scheduled time off, balances are deducted in a fixed order.

OrderBalance usedWhen
1Paid annual leaveFirst, always
2Monthly days offIf annual leave is exhausted
3Unpaid leaveLast resort, if both are gone

Emergencies: notify your lead as soon as reasonably possible. Documentation may be required on your return.

Extended absence: anything past 3 consecutive working days, or arising from special circumstances, is handled case by case and needs approval from both your direct lead and management.

3.6

How requests are answered

This clause is for Julius and the SPVs. Everyone else can skip it, though there is no harm in knowing how the other side works.

The rule

Every request is answered by setting the Approval field. The status follows from it automatically and the request clears out of the approval queue. Statuses are never set by hand.

In GoodDay
What you setStatus becomesWhy it matters
Approve a sick daySICKKeeps illness separate from leave in the record
Approve a day offDAY OFFUnpaid unless you mark it paid
Mark as paid leavePAID LEAVEWorks on a sick day as well as a day off. Payroll depends on this one, because it is the only place paid leave is separated from an ordinary unpaid absence
DeclineDECLINEDCovers both sick and day off

You do not have to remember to look. Every sick day and every day off also lands in Julius's Action Required queue the moment it is filed, so it surfaces in My Work without opening this view at all. Awaiting approval is the place to see them all together; Action Required is what stops one being missed.

Self-approval is watched, not blocked

GoodDay cannot stop a person approving their own day off. Instead, any change to an approval field raises an alert. It is not an accusation. It is what lets us say the record is trustworthy without locking anything down.

Reading one person's month. Open All records. It is grouped by the name each person picked, so their days sit together. The same person twice on one date is a mistake worth correcting, because it reads as two days worked.

Who is free for the next job

That question is answered on the Projects board, not here. On whose desk shows what is waiting on each person today, Workload shows what they are already committed to. Both are in the Leadership workflow guide.

Part 4

Compensation

What attendance turns into at the end of the month, and the allowances that sit alongside it.

4.1

Pay schedule

The rule

All team members are paid monthly. Salaries are processed at the end of each month from the attendance data locked at the 25th cut-off. Your pay slip, including any attendance-related adjustments, is sent to your registered email.

Keep your banking details on file accurate and current. If there is ever a delay or a discrepancy, notify management immediately rather than waiting for the next cycle.

This is the link between Part 1 and Part 4

An unrecorded day is an unpaid day, and after the 25th there is no way to add it. Clause 1.5 is not administrative housekeeping. It is your pay.

4.2

How your salary is set

The rule

Salaries are set on role, scope of responsibilities, and contribution to the team. We aim for rates competitive with the international standard of work we deliver. Any change to your compensation, including promotions, raises and structural adjustments, is communicated in writing.

We are transparent about pay. If you have questions about your salary or how it is structured, ask management directly. Formal reviews happen twice a year and are the usual place compensation changes are discussed.

4.3

Salary restructuring, effective March 2026

The rule

Two structural changes apply to every employee.

ChangeWhat it means
income tax withholding Income tax is withheld in accordance with Indonesian tax law. Under the TER system in force since January 2024, monthly withholding runs January through November, with a full progressive reconciliation in December
Components restructured The breakdown of each employee's compensation has been adjusted by role. A personalised breakdown is sent to your registered email. Review it and confirm receipt

The December figure will look different from the other eleven months. That is the reconciliation, not an error. If your own breakdown does not match what lands in your account, raise it with management the same month.

4.4

Overtime compensation

The rule

The studio operates on a flexible, output-based model, and trusts you to manage your own time. Where a project demands more than the standard workload, statutory overtime entitlements for employees under formal agreements are defined in your individual contract and honoured in full.

The route for raising it is in clause 2.3. Bring evidence, and expect a review of how work is allocated rather than a one-off payment argument.

4.5

Religious holiday allowance

The rule

Every employee is entitled to the religious holiday allowance under Government Regulation No. 36/2021 and Minister of Manpower Regulation No. 6/2016, after 1 month of continuous service, whether permanent or fixed-term.

Length of serviceWhat you receive
12 months or moreOne full month's salary
Under 12 monthsProrated: (months of service ÷ 12) × one month's salary

Paid no later than 7 days before your primary religious holiday, in a single lump sum. Instalments are not permitted. The allowance is paid according to your registered religion, and Indonesia officially recognises six: Islam, Protestantism, Catholicism, Hinduism, Buddhism and Confucianism, each with its corresponding primary holiday.

Tax: The holiday allowance is subject to income tax withholding. It is not subject to BPJS contributions.

Late payment carries a statutory fine

A late holiday allowance is subject to a fine of 5% of the total owed, under Government Regulation No. 36/2021. Planaria Studio is committed to full and timely compliance, and this clause exists so you know the obligation is real.

Part 5

Quick reference

Everything above, compressed to what you need on an ordinary Tuesday.

5.1

The short list

  • Sunday is off. File nothing.
  • Working today? Daily Standup, before you start.
  • Not working today? Time Off Request instead. Never both.
  • Pick your own name. The form will happily file your day against someone else.
  • Copy the job name off the Projects board. It becomes the title of the record.
  • A second job today goes in Other work today, not a second standup.
  • One standup covers one day. One time off request can cover several.
  • A day off needs two working days' notice. Sick can be the same morning.
  • Write a reason. It is the only place Julius can see one.
  • Paid leave is never automatic. Julius marks it, payroll reads it.
  • Everything closes on the 25th at 23:59 WIB. Nothing is fixed afterwards.
  • Sundays come out of your 8. Never more than 9 days off in a month.
  • Unused monthly days off become money after N+2. Unused annual leave becomes nothing.
